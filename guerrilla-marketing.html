The problem with generic local ads

Most local advertising wastes your budget on the wrong people.

Billboards, boulevard flags, and broad social ads put your message in front of anyone — most of whom will never set foot near your store. Proximity marketing fixes the targeting problem at the source: real reach, near your real location, timed to your real event.

PROXIMITY

Verified nearby reach

Your message lands with real households near your physical location — not a loosely defined "area" or a randomly targeted ad audience.

TIMING

Event-timed delivery

Campaigns land in the days before your sale, anniversary, or launch — when the message actually matters, not weeks too early or too late.

PROOF

Delivery data included

Every campaign comes back with a map and a delivery report — real numbers on real reach, not an impression estimate.

How it works

Three steps. Zero wasted reach.

01

Define your radius and timing

Tell us your location, your event date, and how far out you want to reach. We map the radius with you.

02

We deliver directly to nearby households

Your promo material reaches verified households in that radius, timed to land in the days before your event.

03

You get a map and a delivery report

See exactly what was delivered, where, and when — real data to judge the campaign, not a vague reach estimate.
