Mystery Shopping in Pakistan

See your outlets the way a customer does.

Request Mystery ShoppingAll Research and Insights formats

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

Mystery shopping sends trained shoppers into your outlets as ordinary customers to score what they experience: how staff greet them, whether the product is in stock and how long service takes. You receive a checklist report with photos where allowed.

Works best when

  • Retail, restaurant, bank-branch and service outlets with many locations.
  • Brands that depend on franchisees or distributors to deliver an experience.
  • Launches where staff training matters.
  • Companies that want a neutral view of service quality.

How a typical run goes

  1. Checklist

    We agree the score sheet: greeting, product knowledge, availability, cleanliness, speed and upsell, plus any scenario to follow.

  2. Shopper briefing

    Shoppers are briefed on the scenario and given a fixed set of questions so visits are comparable.

  3. Visit

    Shoppers make the visit, complete a checklist right after and photograph where permitted.

  4. Report

    You receive scored checklists and a summary, with highlights and gaps by outlet.

What we need from you

  • List of outlets and the scenarios to follow.
  • The checklist items you want scored.
  • Any items shoppers must not do, for example make payments.
  • How results will be shared with outlet staff.

What you receive

  • A scored checklist for each visit.
  • Photos where the outlet allows.
  • A summary of strengths and gaps by outlet.
  • Recommended follow-ups for training.

Honest limits

  • One visit is a single experience, not an average. Repeat visits show a pattern.
  • Scores are most useful when shared as coaching, not blame.
  • Shoppers do not complete purchases or give personal data unless the scope says so.

Pricing

A Research Sprint starts from $450 and covers a focused study with charts, raw data and a summary report. Recurring work such as price tracking is quoted as a monthly retainer. Draft indicative pricing; final scope and price are confirmed on your strategy call.

Frequently asked questions

Do outlet staff know about it?

No. Shoppers act as ordinary customers. How results are shared is your decision.

Can shoppers make purchases?

Only if the scope says so, and the cost is agreed in advance.

How many visits do we need?

More visits give a more reliable view. We recommend a number during scoping.

Is it legal to photograph in store?

We photograph only where the outlet allows it.
