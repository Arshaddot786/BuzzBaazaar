Pack and Label Preference Test

Choose the pack your shoppers actually pick.

Request a Pack TestAll Research and Insights formats

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

A pack and label preference test shows three designs to 80 to 120 people and records which each person would pick and why. You receive a preference breakdown with comments. It is a low-cost check before you commit to a print run.

Works best when

  • You have two or three design directions and cannot decide.
  • You are localising a global pack for Pakistan.
  • You are changing a pack and want to avoid confusing existing buyers.
  • You need evidence to settle an internal debate.

How a typical run goes

  1. Prepare designs

    You supply three designs, mocked up as printed cards or real packs. We keep size and colour accurate.

  2. Test

    Surveyors show the designs in rotated order, so position does not bias the result, and ask which people would pick and why.

  3. Probe

    We ask what each design says about the product, price and quality.

  4. Report

    We report preference shares, key reasons and any design that confuses.

What we need from you

  • Three designs, print-ready or mocked up.
  • The audience and the category.
  • What you want to learn: preference, clarity or premium feel.
  • Any legal text that must stay on the pack.

What you receive

  • Preference shares for each design.
  • Reasons grouped by theme.
  • A note on any design that people misread.
  • A short method note with sample size.

Honest limits

  • Preference on a card is not the same as choice on a crowded shelf. Pair with a shelf audit if shelf impact matters.
  • With 80 to 120 people, small gaps between designs are not meaningful. We say when a result is a tie.
  • We test only what you supply. We do not redesign the pack.

Pricing

A Research Sprint starts from $450 and covers a focused study with charts, raw data and a summary report. Recurring work such as price tracking is quoted as a monthly retainer. Draft indicative pricing; final scope and price are confirmed on your strategy call.

Frequently asked questions

Why 80 to 120 people?

It gives a practical read on direction at a low cost. We say clearly when the gap between designs is too small to call.

Can we test more than three designs?

Yes, with a larger sample. The scope sets the number.

Do you show the real pack or a card?

Either. A real pack is better where touch and size matter.

Can we test in several cities?

Yes. Street surveys run in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad.
