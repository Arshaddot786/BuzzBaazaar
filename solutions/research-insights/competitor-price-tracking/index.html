Competitor Price Tracking in Karachi

Know what your competitors charge, week after week.

Request Price TrackingAll Research and Insights formats

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

Competitor price tracking is a retainer service: each week or month, our auditors visit 15 to 30 Karachi stores, record the shelf price of your product and your competitors’ products, and send you an Excel or PDF report. It turns scattered store prices into a clear trend.

Works best when

  • You sell in a price-sensitive category and prices move often.
  • You need to react to promotions and price cuts.
  • You are negotiating with retailers and need evidence.
  • You want a long-run record of price changes.

How a typical run goes

  1. Basket and stores

    We agree the products to track and the 15 to 30 stores to visit, and keep the list stable so trends are real.

  2. Visit rhythm

    Auditors visit weekly or monthly on the same days and record price, promotions and out-of-stock.

  3. Quality check

    We check outliers against photos before they go into the report.

  4. Report

    You receive an Excel file or PDF with prices by store, changes since last time and notable promotions.

What we need from you

  • The product basket, with pack sizes.
  • Store list or criteria.
  • Report format: Excel or PDF.
  • Frequency: weekly or monthly.

What you receive

  • Prices by store and product.
  • Change since the last visit, with promotions noted.
  • Photo checks for unusual prices.
  • A trend chart that grows with each visit.

Honest limits

  • Only shelf prices are tracked, not discounts agreed off the shelf.
  • Store lists work best when kept stable, so we avoid frequent changes.
  • Currently Karachi-based.
