Street Intercept Surveys in Pakistan

Ask real people a few good questions, and get answers you can use.

Request a SurveyAll Research and Insights formats

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

A street intercept survey stops people in a public place and asks 5 to 10 short questions. Trained surveyors record answers on phones or forms, and you receive charts, raw data and a summary. It is the fastest way to hear what real people in a Pakistani city think about your product, price or idea.

Works best when

  • You need directional answers in days, not months.
  • You are testing a message, a name or an offer before launch.
  • Your audience is public-facing, such as students, shoppers or commuters.
  • You want quotes and reactions as well as numbers.

How a typical run goes

  1. Question design

    We write 5 to 10 neutral questions with you. Leading questions are removed, and answer choices are tested before fieldwork.

  2. Sites and quotas

    We choose locations that match your audience and set simple quotas, such as age bands, so the sample is not one type of person.

  3. Fieldwork

    Surveyors approach people, explain the purpose, ask for consent and record answers. Refusals are logged.

  4. Analysis

    We clean the data, build charts and write the key findings, with a method note.

What we need from you

  • The decision the survey should inform.
  • Your target audience and locations.
  • Any concept, name or pack to show respondents.
  • Languages needed, for example Urdu and English.

What you receive

  • Charts and a written summary.
  • Raw data in Excel on request.
  • A method note with sample size, locations, dates and limits.
  • Selected verbatim quotes, with consent.

Honest limits

  • A street sample is a practical field sample. We do not describe it as nationally representative.
  • Short questionnaires suit simple questions. Complex studies need a longer format.
  • People who refuse are not represented, and we say so in the method note.

Frequently asked questions

How many people will you survey?

Our planning range is 100 to 200 respondents per day. The final sample size is agreed in the scope and stated in the report.

Can the results be published?

Yes, with the method note. See Surveys and Social Awareness Campaigns if you want a publishable report.

What languages do you survey in?

Urdu and English as standard. Other languages can be arranged on request.

How quickly do we get results?

Timelines depend on sample size and sites. They are confirmed in your scope.
