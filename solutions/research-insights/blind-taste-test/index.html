Blind Product Taste Test

Take the label away and see which one people prefer.

Request a Taste TestAll Research and Insights formats

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

A blind taste test serves your product and competing products in identical, unbranded cups and asks people which they prefer. Because nobody can see the brand, the result is about taste alone. BuzzBaazaar runs the test with consent and allergy disclosure and reports the findings plainly.

Works best when

  • Food and drink brands that believe their product tastes better.
  • Reformulations, where you need to know if people notice the change.
  • Launches against a strong local incumbent.
  • Brands that want an honest answer before spending on claims.

How a typical run goes

  1. Test design

    We agree the products, how they are coded, the serving size and the question: preference, intensity or likelihood to buy.

  2. Safety first

    Participants are told what the products contain, asked about allergies and give consent before tasting. Products are stored and served cleanly.

  3. Fieldwork

    Surveyors serve coded samples in random order and record preferences and comments.

  4. Report

    We report preference shares, comments and the method, including sample size and limits.

Planning notes

FormatCoded, unbranded samples served in random order
SafetyConsent and allergy disclosure before tasting
OutputPreference shares, comments, method note

What we need from you

  • Your product and the competitors, in date, with ingredients.
  • Serving and storage instructions.
  • Approval of the method and of any claim you may make afterwards.
  • Sites suitable for a clean, shaded serving point.

What you receive

  • Preference shares with a method note.
  • Verbatim comments, with consent.
  • Photos of the setup, without faces unless people agree.
  • A caution list: what the result can and cannot support in advertising.

Honest limits

  • A small street sample does not support a claim such as “preferred by Pakistan”. We advise on what the evidence can say.
  • Temperature, serving order and cup size all affect results, so we control them and record them.
  • If the result goes against you, we report it as it is.

Frequently asked questions

Can we use the result in advertising?

Only within what the evidence supports, and subject to advertising rules. We advise on safe wording.

How do you manage allergies?

We disclose ingredients, ask about allergies and do not serve anyone who has declared one.

Do you include competitor products?

Yes. You choose them, and we buy them at retail.

Can you test non-food products?

Yes, for example scent or texture tests, with the same consent approach.
