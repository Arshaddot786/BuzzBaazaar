Retail Shelf Audit in Karachi

See what your product looks like on the shelf, store by store.

Request a Shelf AuditAll Research and Insights formats

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

A retail shelf audit sends trained auditors to 20 to 50 Karachi stores to photograph and record where your product sits, whether it is in stock, what it costs and what stands next to it. You receive a photo-backed report within 48 hours of the visits.

Works best when

  • You have a distributor and want to check that stock reaches shelves.
  • You paid for a promotion and need to see if it is in store.
  • You want to compare your placement and price with competitors.
  • You are about to meet a retailer and need evidence.

How a typical run goes

  1. Store list

    You give us the list of stores or a store type to sample. We agree the number and areas.

  2. Checklist

    We agree what to record: availability, facings, shelf position, price, promotions and nearby competitors.

  3. Visits

    Auditors visit, photograph the shelf and record the checklist on the spot, with store permission where needed.

  4. Report

    You receive a report with photo evidence and a summary within 48 hours of the visits.

What we need from you

  • Store list or criteria.
  • Your product list, with codes or pack images.
  • The checklist items that matter most.
  • Any competitors to record.

What you receive

  • A photo-backed audit report.
  • Availability and price tables by store.
  • A map-ready store list.
  • A list of gaps to fix with your distributor.

Honest limits

  • Audits are currently Karachi-based.
  • A store audit shows one moment. Repeating it shows trend.
  • Store staff may limit photography. We respect that and record what we can.

Pricing

A Research Sprint starts from $450 and covers a focused study with charts, raw data and a summary report. Recurring work such as price tracking is quoted as a monthly retainer. Draft indicative pricing; final scope and price are confirmed on your strategy call.
