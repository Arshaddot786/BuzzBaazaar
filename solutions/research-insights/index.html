Research and Insights

Real-world data from the streets and shelves of Pakistan.

Request ResearchSee packages

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

BuzzBaazaar’s research and insights service collects field data in Pakistan: street surveys, price and packaging tests, brand perception studies, retail shelf audits, competitor price tracking and mystery shopping. You receive charts, raw data and a clear report you can act on.

Who it is for

  • FMCG brands testing price, packaging or taste.
  • Brands tracking presence and pricing in retail stores.
  • Marketers measuring awareness, recall and trust.
  • Companies checking service quality at outlets.

What you get

  • Street Intercept Surveys: 5 to 10 questions, planning range of 100 to 200 respondents per day, with charts and raw data.
  • Price Comparison Survey: competing products at different price points, delivered as an Excel report with charts.
  • Brand Perception Survey: awareness, recall, trust and association across locations, delivered as a branded 5-page report.
  • Blind Product Taste Test: unbranded testing against competitors, with food safety, consent and allergy disclosures.
  • Pack and Label Preference Test: 3 designs compared with 80 to 120 respondents and a preference breakdown.
  • Retail Shelf Audit: 20 to 50 Karachi stores with photo evidence and a report within 48 hours.
  • Competitor Price Tracking: weekly or monthly visits to 15 to 30 stores, as Excel or PDF, on a monthly retainer.
  • Mystery Shopping: trained shoppers score staff behaviour, availability and service speed, with a photo and checklist report.

How it works

  1. Scope

    We agree your questions, audience and locations.

  2. Fieldwork

    Trained surveyors and shoppers collect data and photo evidence.

  3. Analysis

    We clean the data and build charts and findings.

  4. Report

    You receive the report, raw data and a debrief.

Frequently asked questions

Are your samples statistically representative?

Street samples are practical field samples. We state the sample size and method in every report, and we do not describe them as nationally representative unless the method supports it.

How fast is a shelf audit?

Reports are delivered within 48 hours of the store visits, depending on scope.

Can you run recurring price tracking?

Yes, weekly or monthly on a retainer.

How much does research cost?

A Research Sprint starts from $450. Final scope and price are confirmed on your strategy call.

Do you handle taste tests safely?

Yes. Taste tests require consent, allergy disclosure and your approval of the product and method.
