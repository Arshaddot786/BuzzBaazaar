Viral Content Engine

Short-form street content, produced in Pakistan and delivered ready to post.

Book a Strategy CallSee packages

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

The Viral Content Engine is BuzzBaazaar’s monthly production service for short-form video. We shoot street interviews, public reactions and creator-style clips in Pakistan, edit them for each platform and deliver them with your logo and music, so your channels stay active without you running a production team.

Who it is for

  • AI, SaaS and app brands that need a steady stream of short video for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and Facebook.
  • Consumer brands that want authentic street reactions to a product, price or idea.
  • Agencies that need a reliable offshore production partner.
  • Startups that want proof of audience interest before spending on ads.

What you get

  • 5 to 8 edited clips per shoot day, formatted for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and Facebook, with your logo and music.
  • Clips delivered within 48 hours of each shoot day where included in scope.
  • A mass-communication interviewer and a videographer on location, with your brand as the backdrop.
  • Topic planning: currently debated questions on relationships, money, careers, city life and your product category.
  • Captions, thumbnails and a posting plan.
  • A short reporting note on what was shot, where and how it performed on your channels.

How it works

  1. Brief and topics

    You share your brand, audience and goal. We propose viral topics and a question list for approval.

  2. Shoot

    Our interviewer and videographer shoot at universities, food streets and public spaces, with permissions arranged.

  3. Edit and deliver

    We cut platform-ready clips with your logo and music and deliver them within 48 hours of the shoot day.

  4. Review and repeat

    We review what worked and plan the next batch. Monthly plans repeat this cycle.

Packages and options

Content Spark

1 day, 5 to 8 clips, 1 location, 1 interviewer and 1 videographer, clips within 48 hours.

Content Series

3 days, 20+ clips, 3 locations, 3 viral topics and a compiled brand highlight reel.

Viral City Tour

7 days, 40+ clips across 5 to 7 Karachi locations, enough content for about 2 months, plus an impact report.

Where we deliver

Shoots run in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad. The Viral City Tour format is planned for Karachi locations.

Reporting and proof

  • A delivery list of every clip with the shoot day and location.
  • Raw footage handover on request.
  • A short summary of topics tested and audience reactions.
  • An impact report with the Viral City Tour.
