Creator and Influencer Campaigns

The right Pakistani creators for your brief, managed end to end.

Request Creator MatchingSee packages

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What it is

BuzzBaazaar’s creator service matches your brand with Pakistani YouTubers, TikTokers and Instagram creators by niche, audience and content style, then manages outreach, briefing, scheduling and delivery tracking. We match on fit, not follower count alone.

Who it is for

  • Brands entering Pakistan that need local voices and credibility.
  • Consumer, food, fashion, tech and app brands looking for authentic content.
  • Teams that want ongoing creator collaborations across weeks or months.
  • Agencies that need a managed creator network in Pakistan.

What you get

  • Creator matching by industry, niche and audience fit.
  • Outreach, negotiation support and briefing handled by our team.
  • Scheduling of store visits, brand pranks, social experiments and content shoots.
  • Delivery tracking so you see what is published and when.
  • Ongoing collaborations, where a creator produces content for your brand over weeks or months.
  • A campaign summary at the end.

How it works

  1. Brief and shortlist

    You describe your brand, goal and audience. We propose creator categories and a shortlist for approval.

  2. Outreach and briefing

    We contact creators, confirm availability and rates, and brief them on messaging and disclosure.

  3. Production and publishing

    Creators produce content. We schedule shoots and track delivery against the plan.

  4. Review

    We share a summary of what was published and recommend next steps.

Packages and options

Categories

Prank, food, fashion, social experiment, entertainment and comedy, podcast and tech. Other niches such as beauty, fitness, travel and gaming can be sourced on request.

Cities

Creators are available in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Faisalabad.

Where we deliver

Our creator network covers Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Faisalabad.

Reporting and proof

  • Delivery tracking for every agreed post.
  • Links to published content.
  • A summary of platforms, dates and formats.
  • Creator names and follower figures are shared only with approval and verified before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

How do you choose creators?

We match by niche, audience and content style. Follower count is one factor, not the only one.

Can I see the creator list?

We share a shortlist for your brief with verified details. We do not publish creator names or follower counts without approval.

How are creator fees decided?

Fees depend on the creator, the platform and the scope. We confirm them with you before any commitment.

Will sponsored posts be disclosed?

Yes. Sponsored content is clearly disclosed in line with platform rules.

Can creators work with us over several months?

Yes. We can set up recurring collaborations that run over weeks or months.
