Brand Ambassadors and Event Staff

Briefed, presentable teams for activations, expos and roadshows.

Request StaffSee packages

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What it is

BuzzBaazaar supplies sourced, vetted and briefed brand ambassadors, expo and booth staff, promoters, part-time teams and roadshow crews across Pakistan. Each team is managed by a point of contact throughout your event, and is scaled from a single trade-show host to a full roadshow crew.

Who it is for

  • Brands exhibiting at trade shows and expos.
  • Retailers and FMCG brands running in-store promotions.
  • Organisers of product launches, roadshows and mall promotions.
  • Companies that need staff for a day, a weekend or a full campaign.

What you get

  • Brand ambassadors: briefed representatives for activations and launches.
  • Expo and booth staff who greet visitors, demo products and capture leads.
  • Promoters and samplers for in-store demos and sampling.
  • Part-time and contractual staff for a day, a weekend or a campaign.
  • Volunteer and CSR teams for community and awareness events.
  • Roadshow crews for several venues or cities.

How it works

  1. Share event details

    Date, location, role type and team size.

  2. Sourcing and vetting

    We source and vet staff and match them to your brand.

  3. Briefing

    Staff are briefed on product, messaging and event flow.

  4. Event day

    The team arrives presentable and on time, managed by a point of contact.

Frequently asked questions

How do you vet staff?

We source candidates, check suitability for your brand and brief them before the event. A point of contact manages the team on the day.

What does it cost?

Basic staff start from $20 per person per day and premium English-fluent staff from $35. Final pricing is confirmed on your strategy call.

Can you supply staff in several cities?

Yes. Roadshow crews can cover multiple venues and cities.

Can we book for one day?

Yes. You can book for a day, a weekend or a full campaign.

Do you provide uniforms?

Branded uniforms and print can be arranged through our production partners as part of the scope.
