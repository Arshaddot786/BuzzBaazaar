Proximity and Guerrilla Marketing

Reach the customers already near you before your next sale.

Plan a Local CampaignSee packages

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What it is

Proximity and guerrilla marketing is hyperlocal, event-timed marketing delivered to verified households near a client’s location. You give us a location, an event date and a reach radius, and we deliver your promotion before the event and return a map and a delivery report with actual figures.

Who it is for

  • Retailers planning anniversary sales, clearances or end-of-season sales.
  • Brands opening a new store.
  • Businesses running seasonal promotions or product launches.
  • Organisations marking a brand milestone.

What you get

  • A radius mapped with you around your location.
  • Delivery to verified nearby households, timed before your event.
  • A map showing where delivery happened.
  • A delivery report with actual figures, not an impression estimate.
  • Planning support on timing and materials.

How it works

  1. Define radius and timing

    You share your location, event date and how far out to reach. We map the radius with you.

  2. Delivery

    Promotional material reaches households in that radius before the event.

  3. Reporting

    You receive a map and a delivery report showing what was delivered, where and when.

Packages and options

Use cases

Anniversary sales, new store launches, clearance and end-of-season events, seasonal promotions, product launches and brand milestones.

Where we deliver

Karachi first, with Lahore and Islamabad available on request.

Reporting and proof

  • A delivery map.
  • A report with actual delivery figures.
  • Dates and times of delivery.
  • Notes on any exceptions.

Frequently asked questions

What is proximity marketing?

It is marketing delivered directly to households near a specific location, timed to an event such as a sale or launch.

How is delivery reported?

You receive a map and a report of actual delivery figures, not an impression estimate.

What do you need from us?

Your location, the event date and the radius you want to reach.

Is pricing fixed?

No. Pricing depends on radius, volume and materials, and is confirmed on your strategy call.

Which cities are covered?

Karachi first, with Lahore and Islamabad on request.
