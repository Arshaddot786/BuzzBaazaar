Rooftop Car Fleet

A branded convertible that stops the street, filmed from the first reveal.

Book Fleet AvailabilitySee packages

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What it is

The BuzzBaazaar rooftop car fleet is a set of fully wrapped convertibles, led by an owned Daihatsu Copen with a retractable hardtop, that run branded activations in Karachi. The car parks roof-closed, a crowd builds, a countdown plays and the roof opens, all filmed, with QR lead capture for every participant.

Who it is for

  • Brands launching a product or app in Karachi.
  • Companies exhibiting at expos and public events.
  • Teams that want a memorable visual for social content.
  • Brands combining sampling, quizzes and content in one activation.

What you get

  • A full brand wrap on hood, doors, roof panels, mirrors and bumper strips.
  • The rooftop reveal, filmed each time.
  • QR lead capture collecting name, phone and city, with consent and disclosure.
  • A trained ambassador at each car, with a live quiz or sampling.
  • Photo reports and a brand reel within 48 hours, depending on the package.
  • A one-time wrap setup fee per branded vehicle, quoted separately.

How it works

  1. Plan

    We agree the package, locations and date and design the wrap.

  2. Wrap

    The Copen and any rented convertibles are wrapped and the QR form is built.

  3. Reveal

    The car runs the activation with live updates and a videographer.

  4. Report

    You receive photos, the reel and the lead list.

Packages and options

Solo Spark

1 car, 1 location, 4 to 6 hour activation, 1 ambassador, live quiz or sampling, QR lead capture and a same-day photo report.

Triple Rooftop

3 cars, 3 locations, 3 ambassadors, a QR trail where scanning all three cars wins a grand prize, a selfie zone per location, social story templates, an end-of-day photo report and a 60-second brand reel in 48 hours.

Citywide Blitz

5 cars across 5 Karachi zones, 5 ambassadors, an optional City Chase posted to Instagram Stories, a dedicated videographer all day, a 1,000 QR lead target, a 1-minute reel in 48 hours and CSR documentation.

Where we deliver

Karachi only. Convertibles #2 and #3 in the larger packages are rented, with removable vinyl wraps. Fleet details for Lahore and Islamabad are not available.

Reporting and proof

  • Same-day or end-of-day photo report, depending on package.
  • Brand reel within 48 hours.
  • QR lead list in Excel, where consent allows.
  • Participation figures and CSR documentation where relevant.

Frequently asked questions

Which car do you use?

The lead vehicle is an owned Daihatsu Copen with a retractable hardtop. Larger packages add rented convertibles with removable wraps.

Is the fleet available outside Karachi?

No. The fleet runs in Karachi only.

What is the wrap fee?

Wrap setup is a separate one-time fee per branded vehicle, quoted on your strategy call.

How many leads will we get?

Lead counts are targets that depend on location and crowd. We report the actual number.

How soon can a car be on the street?

Typically 3 to 7 days after the brief is approved, depending on availability, permits and production.
