Street Product Sampling in Pakistan

Put the product in people’s hands where they already walk.

Plan a Sampling CampaignAll Field and BTL Activations formats

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

Street product sampling places 2 to 3 trained ambassadors at a busy public site to hand out your product, explain it in a sentence and capture feedback or leads. BuzzBaazaar plans the sites, supplies the staff and the signage, and sends a photo report each day.

Works best when

  • Launches of food, drink, personal care or household products.
  • Products that people judge in one taste, sniff or touch.
  • Brands testing which locations bring the best response.
  • Campaigns that need a quick read on first reactions.

How a typical run goes

  1. Site plan

    We choose sites by audience: university gates, malls, food streets or office clusters. We confirm permissions before the date.

  2. Briefing

    Ambassadors learn the product line, the one-sentence pitch and the answers to common questions from your fact sheet.

  3. Sample

    Ambassadors hand out samples from a branded table or bag, ask one feedback question and offer a QR card where leads are wanted.

  4. Report

    A photo report arrives each day, with the sample count and notes on what people said.

Planning notes

Planning range300 to 500 samples per day per location
Typical crew2 to 3 ambassadors per location
ReportingActual counts, with a photo report each day

What we need from you

  • Sample product in the quantity agreed, in date, and a handling note.
  • The fact sheet and any allergen or ingredient notes.
  • Brand artwork for the table, banner and cards.
  • The feedback question you want asked.

What you receive

  • A daily photo report.
  • Actual samples handed out, by site.
  • A short feedback summary.
  • QR leads in Excel where consent allows.

Honest limits

  • The planning range is not a promise. Weather, site and product all change the number, and we report the real count.
  • Food and drink sampling needs clean handling and allergen disclosure.
  • Street feedback is quick and informal. For structured findings use a Research Sprint.

Pricing

This format can be scoped as a stand-alone activation, inside the Launch Pack (from $1,800) or inside Expo and Event Presence (from $950). Ambassadors start from $20 per person per day. Print, signage and prizes depend on your design and quantities. Draft indicative pricing; final scope and price are confirmed on your strategy call.

Frequently asked questions

How many samples can one team hand out?

Our planning range is 300 to 500 per day per location. We report the actual number.

Who supplies the samples?

The brand supplies them. Print and signage can be arranged through our production partners.

Can we do sampling in Lahore and Islamabad?

Yes. Street sampling runs in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad.

Can we collect feedback at the same time?

Yes. One short question is easy to add, and a longer study is better as a Research Sprint.
