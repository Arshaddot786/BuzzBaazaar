Leaflet and Flyer Distribution in Pakistan

Print in the right hands, with proof along the way.

What it is

Leaflet and flyer distribution puts printed material in front of a chosen audience at set locations, at events or door to door. BuzzBaazaar plans the sites, briefs the team, sends photo proof every 30 minutes and reports the actual number distributed. It is the simplest low-cost way to reach a local audience and carry people to a QR code.

Works best when

  • Local promotions, openings and offers within a few kilometres.
  • Event announcements and expo footfall drives.
  • Campaigns that point people to a QR code or a short link.
  • Testing which areas respond before bigger spend.

How a typical run goes

  1. Design and print

    We confirm the leaflet size, language and QR code, and arrange printing through our production partners.

  2. Site plan

    We choose sites and times to match your audience, such as a campus at class change or a market on a weekend.

  3. Distribute

    Ambassadors hand leaflets to people in person, not by dropping them on the ground. Photo proof is sent every 30 minutes.

  4. Report

    You receive the actual count distributed, by site and shift, and QR scan data from your own link.

What we need from you

  • Final artwork, or a brief for design.
  • The QR destination and any offer code.
  • Audience and area targets.
  • Quantity and dates.

What you receive

  • Photo proof through each shift.
  • An actual-count distribution report.
  • A site-by-site note on response.
  • QR scan data if you use a trackable link.

Honest limits

  • Scan rates on printed flyers are usually low. Use them for reach and a clear call to action.
  • Distribution must follow site rules. We avoid littering and unpermitted sites.
  • A guaranteed count must be written into the contract. Without it, we report the actual number.

Pricing

This format can be scoped as a stand-alone activation, inside the Launch Pack (from $1,800) or inside Expo and Event Presence (from $950). Ambassadors start from $20 per person per day. Print, signage and prizes depend on your design and quantities. Draft indicative pricing; final scope and price are confirmed on your strategy call.
