ORS and Electrolyte Giveaway Campaigns

You supply the product. We put it in the right hands, safely.

What it is

An ORS and electrolyte giveaway is a distribution campaign for a hydration product, usually timed to hot weather. You supply the product; BuzzBaazaar distributes it through trained ambassadors, films it and captures leads where consent allows. Compliance, consent and safety guidance are confirmed with you before anything is handed out.

Works best when

  • Hydration, sports and wellness brands launching or reintroducing a product.
  • Campaigns timed to heat, Ramadan evenings, sports events or marathons.
  • Pharmacy and retail brands that want trial before purchase.
  • Brands that need documented distribution for a partner or regulator.

How a typical run goes

  1. Compliance first

    You confirm the product is legally sold in Pakistan, correctly labelled and in date. We agree what staff may and may not say.

  2. Brief the team

    Ambassadors learn the product facts from your approved sheet and are told not to give medical advice or dosage guidance.

  3. Distribute

    Ambassadors hand out the product at agreed sites and offer a QR card for an offer or a sign-up. Distribution is tracked by site and hour.

  4. Report

    You receive a count of units given, photos, clips and a lead list, where consent allows.

Planning notes

Typical crew2 to 4 ambassadors per site
Reported metricsUnits distributed (counted), leads where consent allows
Run lengthSingle event to multi-week plan

What we need from you

  • Product, in date, with batch details.
  • The approved fact sheet, and any warnings that must be said.
  • Confirmation that the product may be handed out publicly.
  • The lead-capture offer, if any.

What you receive

  • A distribution log by site and hour.
  • Photo and video documentation, with consent.
  • A QR lead list in Excel, where consent allows.
  • A short note on questions people asked, which feeds your next brief.

Honest limits

  • We do not give medical advice. Staff repeat the label and your approved facts only.
  • Handing out products to children, or to people who say they are unwell, follows your safety rules. We agree these up front.
  • Counts are reported as actual figures. No quantity is guaranteed unless it is written into the contract.

Pricing

This format can be scoped as a stand-alone activation, inside the Launch Pack (from $1,800) or inside Expo and Event Presence (from $950). Ambassadors start from $20 per person per day. Print, signage and prizes depend on your design and quantities. Draft indicative pricing; final scope and price are confirmed on your strategy call.

Frequently asked questions

Do you supply the product?

No. The brand supplies it. We can help with transport and storage quotes.

Who checks compliance?

You confirm the product is compliant and approved for distribution. We check that the campaign wording stays inside your approved facts.

Can you run it at sports events or marathons?

Yes, subject to event-organiser permission.

How are leads handled?

Only with clear consent and disclosure at the point of capture, and delivered as an Excel list.
