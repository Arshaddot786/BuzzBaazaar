Branded Cap Distribution in Pakistan

A wearable ad people choose to put on.

Plan a Cap CampaignAll Field and BTL Activations formats

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

Branded cap distribution hands out caps printed with your logo at public sites. Anyone who wears one becomes a walking ad, and a QR card or sticker lets you collect leads. BuzzBaazaar arranges the caps through production partners, staffs the handout and reports on it.

Works best when

  • Youth brands, apps, sports and lifestyle brands.
  • Heat-season campaigns, when a cap is genuinely useful.
  • Events and expos where a visible crowd helps.
  • Campaigns that mix a giveaway with lead capture.

How a typical run goes

  1. Design and print

    We confirm the cap design, colours and quantity and arrange production through our print partners. Print lead times are agreed with you.

  2. Handout plan

    We plan sites and shifts so caps reach the right people, not the same few visitors.

  3. Distribute

    Ambassadors hand out caps with a QR card and a one-line offer. A videographer films with consent.

  4. Report

    You receive photos, a count of caps given and a lead list where consent allows.

Planning notes

Cap Spark100 caps, 1 day
Cap Wave300 caps, 3 days, 3 locations
Cap City1,000 caps, 7 days, 7 locations

What we need from you

  • Logo files and any colour limits.
  • Approval of the cap sample before full production.
  • The QR landing page and offer.
  • Dates and cities.

What you receive

  • A count of caps handed out, by site and day.
  • Photo and clip documentation.
  • A QR lead list in Excel, where consent allows.
  • A short report on which sites gave the best response.

Honest limits

  • Lead counts are targets, not guarantees, unless agreed in writing.
  • Production lead time depends on quantity and design.
  • Caps wear out of fashion quickly. A strong design matters more than a big logo.

Pricing

This format can be scoped as a stand-alone activation, inside the Launch Pack (from $1,800) or inside Expo and Event Presence (from $950). Ambassadors start from $20 per person per day. Print, signage and prizes depend on your design and quantities. Draft indicative pricing; final scope and price are confirmed on your strategy call.

Frequently asked questions

What are Cap Spark, Cap Wave and Cap City?

They are three sizes of the same campaign: 100 caps in 1 day, 300 caps in 3 days across 3 locations, and 1,000 caps in 7 days across 7 locations.

Do you make the caps?

We arrange production through print partners and show you a sample for approval.

Are lead numbers guaranteed?

No. They are targets unless agreed in writing, and we report the actual count.

Which cities can you cover?

Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad.
