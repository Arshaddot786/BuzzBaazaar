Field and BTL Activations

Brand moments in public places, run end to end.

Plan an ActivationSee packages

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

Field and BTL activations are on-ground campaigns in public places: quizzes, sampling, heat-relief stations, branded giveaways and distribution. BuzzBaazaar plans, staffs, films and reports on each one in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, so you get real public contact and content from a single partner.

Who it is for

  • FMCG and consumer brands launching or sampling products.
  • Apps and fintech brands seeking sign-ups at university gates and malls.
  • Brands running CSR or heat-relief campaigns.
  • Global brands that need a local operations partner.

What you get

  • Formats listed below, scoped to your goal and location.
  • Trained ambassadors, permissions and production partners for print and signage.
  • Filming of every activation, with edited clips.
  • QR lead capture where consent and disclosure allow.
  • Live updates on the day.
  • A report with participation figures and photos.

How it works

  1. Plan

    We agree format, locations and dates and clear permissions.

  2. Prepare

    We produce print, signage and prizes and brief ambassadors.

  3. Activate

    The team runs the activation with live updates.

  4. Report

    You receive clips, photos, participation figures and leads where collected.

Packages and options

Live Quiz Activation

Brand-themed or general-knowledge quizzes at universities, malls and chowks, with branded prizes.

Misting Fan Zone

A branded cool zone with portable misting fans. A high dwell-time format for summer. Setup, power, water and permissions are confirmed per site.

Chilled Water Station

Branded cooler, canopy and cups at high-footfall locations. Suited to CSR documentation.

Shade Tent Relief Station

A large branded canopy with seating, water and heatstroke awareness cards at busy chowks.

ORS and Electrolyte Giveaway

You supply the product. We distribute it, film it and capture leads, with product compliance, consent and safety guidance confirmed first.

Heatwave Campaigns

Heat-relief and CSR activations planned for Karachi’s summer months.

Street Product Sampling

2 to 3 ambassadors at universities, malls and food streets. Planning range of 300 to 500 samples per day per location, with a photo report.

Door-to-Door Sampling

Residential areas such as DHA, Gulshan, Clifton and North Karachi. Planning range of 200 to 400 homes per day, with proof of delivery.

In-Store Sampling and Demo

Live demos at the point of purchase, subject to retailer permission. Suits food, beverages, personal care and cleaning products.

Copen Sampling Drive

The branded convertible drives through a neighbourhood with the roof open, stopping to hand out samples. Karachi only.

Branded Cap Distribution

100 to 1,000 caps with QR lead capture. Cap Spark: 100 caps, 1 day. Cap Wave: 300 caps, 3 days, 3 locations. Cap City: 1,000 caps, 7 days, 7 locations. Lead counts are targets, not guarantees, unless agreed in writing.

Leaflet and Flyer Distribution

Targeted distribution at set locations, events or door to door, with photo proof every 30 minutes. Counts are guaranteed only when contracted, and we report the actual count.

Where we deliver

Karachi has every format. Lahore and Islamabad are available for brand activations, sampling and street content.

Reporting and proof

  • Photos and clips from each location.
  • Participation figures, peak hours and crowd estimates with method.
  • QR lead list as Excel, where consent allows.
  • CSR documentation for heat-relief and community campaigns.

Frequently asked questions

Which activation formats do you offer?

Quizzes, misting zones, water and shade stations, electrolyte giveaways, street, door-to-door and in-store sampling, branded caps and leaflet distribution.

Do you handle permissions?

Yes. We arrange location permissions as part of production, and timelines depend on the site.

Are the sampling numbers guaranteed?

The figures shown are planning ranges. We report actual numbers, and guaranteed counts apply only when agreed in your contract.

Can we supply our own product and print?

Yes. You supply product where needed, and we can arrange print and signage through our production partners.

Which cities do you cover?

Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, with the rooftop fleet in Karachi only.
