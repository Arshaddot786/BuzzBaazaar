Field and BTL Activations

Door-to-Door Sampling in Karachi

Reach households directly, with proof for every route.

Plan a Home Sampling RouteAll Field and BTL Activations formats

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

Door-to-door sampling takes your product into residential areas of Karachi, home by home, with ambassadors who carry a script, a sample and a way to record each visit. It suits household and family products where the buyer is at home. You receive a route report with proof of delivery.

Works best when

  • Household, baby, food, cleaning and personal-care brands.
  • Launches that need to reach families rather than commuters.
  • Brands comparing response between neighbourhoods.
  • Campaigns that want a documented list of homes reached.

How a typical run goes

  1. Route plan

    We map routes by area, for example DHA, Gulshan, Clifton or North Karachi, and check access rules for gated societies and apartment blocks.

  2. Team

    Ambassadors are briefed on greeting, script, safety and what to do if no one answers. Where needed, they work in pairs.

  3. Visit

    Each visit is logged with time, address block and result: sample delivered, no answer or declined. Photos are taken without showing residents unless they agree.

  4. Report

    You receive a route report with proof of delivery and a short summary of reactions.

Planning notes

Planning range200 to 400 homes per day
Typical crew2 to 4 ambassadors per area
ProofVisit log, photos and a route summary

What we need from you

  • Sample product and any leaflet or offer card.
  • Target areas, or the household profile you want.
  • A script approved by your team.
  • Any restrictions, for example not leaving food at doors.

What you receive

  • A route log with delivered, no-answer and declined counts.
  • Photo proof of delivery.
  • A short summary of resident reactions.
  • QR leads where consent allows.

Honest limits

  • Gated societies and apartment buildings may refuse entry. We plan alternatives and record each refusal.
  • Residents who are out are not counted as reached.
  • Staff safety comes first. We can end a route if conditions are unsafe.

Frequently asked questions

How many homes can you cover in a day?

Our planning range is 200 to 400 homes per day, depending on density and access. We report the actual count.

Do you need permission from housing societies?

Where a society requires it, yes. We request it as part of planning.

What counts as proof of delivery?

A time-stamped visit log and photos of delivery, with residents shown only if they agree.

Does it work outside Karachi?

This format is planned for Karachi. Other cities can be scoped on request.
