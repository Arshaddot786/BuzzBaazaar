Shade Tent Relief Station

A place to sit out of the sun, with your brand overhead.

Plan a Relief StationAll Field and BTL Activations formats

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

A shade tent relief station is a large branded canopy with seating, water and heat-safety awareness cards, placed at a busy chowk, bus stop or market. People rest there, so your brand is visible for much longer than on a billboard. BuzzBaazaar sets it up, staffs it and documents it for your CSR or community report.

Works best when

  • Community and CSR programmes that need real, visible public benefit.
  • Brands that want to be seen across a full day, not a few minutes.
  • Locations where people wait for transport in direct sun.
  • Campaigns that pair well with a chilled water station.

How a typical run goes

  1. Scouting

    We scout the site for foot traffic, safe placement and permission, and plan seating and shade direction.

  2. Install

    A branded canopy, benches or chairs, water and clear signage are installed and checked for stability and wind.

  3. Operate

    Ambassadors keep the area tidy, offer water and hand out awareness cards. The cards are informational and say that serious symptoms need a doctor or emergency service.

  4. Report

    You receive daily photos and a short summary of visitors, peak hours and any community feedback collected with consent.

Planning notes

Typical crew2 to 3 ambassadors, plus a videographer on filming days
Main assetsCanopy, seating, water supply and signage
Reported metricsVisitors (estimated, with method), photos, peak hours

What we need from you

  • Dates, cities and any preferred sites.
  • Brand artwork and the wording of the awareness card.
  • Review of the card against current public-health guidance.
  • Any partner organisations to be named.

What you receive

  • Photo and clip documentation, with consent.
  • An estimated visitor count with the method stated.
  • A one-page CSR summary.
  • Optional QR cards for sign-ups, if consent and disclosure are in place.

Honest limits

  • The station is a shaded rest point, not medical care. Staff are not medical providers and the cards only inform.
  • Wind and storms can force a closure. We plan a backup date and secure the structure.
  • Site permission, not our wish, decides placement.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a medical service?

No. It is shade, seating and water with awareness cards. Staff direct anyone unwell to a doctor or emergency service.

Can partners be named on the tent?

Yes, with their approval, for example a charity or a local group.

How long can a station run?

From a single day to a season, depending on permission and budget.

Can it combine with misting fans?

Yes. See the Misting Fan Zone for the combined setup.
