Live Quiz Activation in Pakistan

A game people stop for, with your brand as the question.

What it is

A live quiz activation is a short, host-led quiz played in a public place. Passers-by answer brand-themed and general-knowledge questions for small prizes, a crowd forms around the game, and your brand sits on the banner, the question cards and the prize table. BuzzBaazaar writes the questions, hosts the game, films it and captures leads with consent.

Works best when

  • You want people to learn one specific fact, such as a feature, a launch date or an offer.
  • The audience is young and social, for example students at university gates or visitors at malls.
  • You need filmable, high-energy content as much as footfall.
  • Your product cannot be sampled (an app or a service) but can be explained in a question.

How a typical run goes

  1. Question set

    We draft 20 to 30 questions. About a third are about your brand, the rest are general knowledge or category topics so the game stays fun. You approve every brand question.

  2. Setup

    A host, a scorer and a videographer set up a branded banner, question cards and a prize table at an agreed spot, with permission arranged beforehand.

  3. Play

    People play in short rounds. Winners choose from the prize table, and everyone is offered a QR code for a draw or an offer, with a clear consent line.

  4. Wrap

    We collect photos and clips, tally participation and send an end-of-day summary.

Planning notes

Typical crewHost, scorer and videographer, plus 1 to 2 ambassadors on busy sites
Typical session4 to 6 hours per location
ParticipationCounted by our team on the day and reported afterwards. We do not promise a number in advance.

What we need from you

  • The brand facts you want taught, and any claims that must not be made.
  • Prizes or a prize budget, and any brand merchandise.
  • Logo files for the banner and cards.
  • The offer or landing page behind the QR code.

What you receive

  • Photos and edited clips of the game.
  • A participation count and peak hours.
  • A QR lead list in Excel, where consent allows.
  • A note on which questions people missed most, which shows what your audience does not yet know about you.

Honest limits

  • Quizzes work where people can stand. A busy junction with nowhere to pause produces little.
  • Keep prize rules simple and public. We recommend against any format that makes people pay to play.
  • Weather and site permissions can move a date, so we agree a backup date up front.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a quiz activation take to set up?

Typically 3 to 7 days after the brief is approved, depending on questions, prizes, print and site permission.

Can the quiz be in Urdu or English?

Either, or both. We set the language to match the site and your audience.

Do you need our approval of the questions?

Yes. You approve every brand question and any claim before the day.

Is this suitable for an app launch?

Yes. Questions can teach one feature and the QR code can point to the store page or a sign-up offer.
