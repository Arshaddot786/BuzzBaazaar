Branded Chilled Water Station

Free cold water, handed out under your name.

Plan a Water StationAll Field and BTL Activations formats

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What it is

A chilled water station is a branded cooler, canopy and supply of cups set up where many people pass in the heat. It is one of the simplest ways to be useful in public, which makes it strong for goodwill and for CSR reporting. BuzzBaazaar sets it up, staffs it and documents every day.

Works best when

  • You want visible goodwill and community benefit more than direct response.
  • Banks, utilities, real estate and telecom brands where trust matters.
  • Campaigns that must show CSR impact in a report.
  • Locations with steady foot traffic, such as chowks, bus stops, markets and shrine or park entrances.

How a typical run goes

  1. Site and permission

    We choose a high-footfall spot and arrange the permission. Site-specific rules shape where the canopy can stand.

  2. Water and supplies

    We agree the water source and cup type with you before the day. We recommend sealed or single-use cups and a clean, approved water supply.

  3. Operate

    Two ambassadors serve, keep the area clean and offer a card with your message. A videographer captures the day with consent.

  4. Document

    We send a daily photo report and a tally of cups served, with the counting method stated.

Planning notes

Typical crew2 ambassadors per station, 1 videographer on filming days
Reported metricsCups served (counted), photos, peak hours
Run lengthSingle day, a week or a season, depending on scope

What we need from you

  • Approval of the water source and the cup design.
  • Brand artwork for the canopy, cooler and cup sleeves.
  • The message on any leaflet or card.
  • Dates and the cities you want covered.

What you receive

  • Daily photo reports with timestamps.
  • A cups-served tally with the counting method.
  • Short vertical clips for social media, with consent from people shown.
  • A CSR documentation pack for heat-relief campaigns.

Honest limits

  • Water hygiene is the first priority. If the supply cannot be verified, we do not run the station.
  • Goodwill campaigns convert slowly. Pair the station with a QR card if you need leads.
  • A beverage brand running its own station should check the site for competing sponsors.

Frequently asked questions

Can we put our product on the station?

Yes, if it fits the setting, for example a branded bottle or cup. For product sampling, see the sampling formats.

How many cups can a station serve?

It depends on site and weather. We report the actual count after each day and do not promise a figure in advance.

Can you run several stations at once?

Yes. Multi-site plans are scoped on your strategy call.

Do you document the work for a CSR report?

Yes. Photo reports and a summary pack are included on request.
