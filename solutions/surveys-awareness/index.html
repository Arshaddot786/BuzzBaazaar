Surveys and Social Awareness Campaigns

Run a survey your brand can own, publish and cite.

Plan a SurveySee packages

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What it is

A survey and awareness campaign is a public or online survey that your brand sponsors and publishes. BuzzBaazaar designs the questions, collects responses in Pakistan and delivers a report with video highlights, charts and pull quotes, giving you both insight and content for PR, social media and sales.

Who it is for

  • Brands that want to own a topic in their category.
  • B2B and SaaS companies publishing a “state of” report.
  • Organisations running CSR or social-awareness campaigns.
  • Marketers who need consumer opinion before a launch.

What you get

  • Survey design with neutral, clear questions.
  • Fieldwork through street intercepts, online collection or both.
  • A branded report with charts and key findings.
  • Video highlights and quotable pull-quote graphics.
  • Raw data on request.
  • Guidance on how to use the results in PR and social media.

How it works

  1. Topic and questions

    We agree the topic, audience and questions with you.

  2. Fieldwork

    Trained surveyors and online forms collect responses at agreed locations.

  3. Analysis

    We clean the data, produce charts and write the findings.

  4. Publish

    You receive the report, video highlights and graphics ready to share.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from market research?

Market research serves your internal decisions. A visibility survey is designed to be published, so the results also become PR and social content.

How many respondents will we get?

It depends on scope and method. We state the sample size and any limits in the report, and we do not present small samples as nationally representative.

Can the survey run online?

Yes. We can combine street intercepts and online collection.

Who owns the results?

The brand sponsoring the survey owns the report and content, subject to the agreed scope.

How much does it cost?

From $550. Final scope and price are confirmed on your strategy call.
