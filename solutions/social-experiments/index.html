Sponsored Social Experiments and Shows

Content people choose to watch, with your brand built in.

Discuss a Sponsored SeriesSee packages

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What it is

A sponsored social experiment is a video or series where real people react to an idea, product or question in public, and your brand sponsors it. BuzzBaazaar writes the concept, hosts and films it in Pakistan, edits it for every platform and plans distribution, so your brand earns visibility and traffic without a traditional ad.

Who it is for

  • Brands that want attention without looking like an ad.
  • AI and software companies launching to a new audience.
  • Consumer brands testing messages on real people.
  • Organisations running social-awareness or CSR campaigns.

What you get

  • Concept and script development on national or international topics.
  • A host or interviewer, location planning and permissions.
  • Filming, editing, platform cut-downs, captions and thumbnails.
  • A publishing plan and distribution through your channels and our creator network.
  • A performance report covering views, shares, comments and clicks or leads where trackable.
  • Clear sponsorship disclosure on every piece.

How it works

  1. Concept

    We propose two or three concepts and scripts around a topic your audience cares about. You approve one.

  2. Production

    We arrange permissions and participants’ consent, then film with a host or interviewer.

  3. Approval

    You review the cut and approve the brand message before anything is published.

  4. Publish and report

    We deliver platform cut-downs, a publishing plan and a performance report.

Packages and options

Formats

Social experiments, street interviews, man-on-the-street opinion shows, challenges and giveaways, ask-the-public series, reaction videos, mini-documentaries and awareness explainers.

Sponsorship models

Single sponsored episode, sponsored series, title sponsor, product placement inside the experiment, branded-backdrop interviews or a co-created concept.

Pricing

Where we deliver

We produce in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad on national topics, and can frame international topics for a Pakistani audience.

Reporting and proof

  • Views, shares, comments and clicks or leads where trackable.
  • A list of every asset delivered.
  • A short learning note on what resonated.
  • Sponsorship disclosure confirmed on each published piece.

Frequently asked questions

What is a sponsored social experiment?

It is a public experiment or interview filmed for social media, sponsored by a brand. It informs or entertains first and carries the brand naturally.

How do you keep content brand-safe?

Sponsorship is clearly disclosed, participants give consent, nothing deceptive is staged as real, and you approve the concept and the final cut before publishing.

Can you cover sensitive topics?

Political and religious topics are handled only with your explicit written approval.

How much does it cost?

A single concept starts from $700 and a monthly series from $1,800. Final scope and price are confirmed on your strategy call.

Where is the content published?

On your channels, our creator partners’ channels or both, according to the distribution plan you approve.
