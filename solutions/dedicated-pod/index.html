Dedicated Offshore Pod

A small team assigned to your brand, working as part of yours.

Discuss a PodSee packages

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What it is

A Dedicated Offshore Pod is a small, part-time team that works only on your brand: a creative lead and editor plus a field and project coordinator. They plan, shoot and edit your monthly content, coordinate creators and run Pakistan activations when you need them, with a weekly report and call.

Who it is for

  • Startups that need a content and field team without hiring.
  • Agencies that want a white-label offshore partner.
  • Brands that need steady monthly output and a point of contact.
  • Teams that want Pakistan field capability on tap.

What you get

  • A named creative lead and editor assigned to your brand.
  • A field and project coordinator for shoots, creators and activations.
  • A monthly content plan agreed with you.
  • Shoots and edits in line with the plan.
  • Creator coordination and Pakistan field activations where needed.
  • A weekly report and call.

How it works

  1. Onboarding

    We learn your brand, tone and goals and set a monthly plan.

  2. Weekly rhythm

    A weekly call reviews progress and priorities.

  3. Production

    The pod shoots, edits and coordinates in line with the plan.

  4. Monthly review

    We review results and adjust the next month’s plan.
