Elite Brand Ambassadors, Models and Actors

Polished on-camera and on-stage talent for premium moments.

Request TalentSee packages

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What it is

Our elite talent tier provides polished, professional and English-fluent brand ambassadors, commercial models, actors, hosts and VIP event staff in Pakistan. We shortlist from portfolios, brief the talent and manage them on the day through one point of contact.

Who it is for

  • Brands running premium launches or expo hospitality.
  • Production teams that need actors or models for ad films and product shoots.
  • Organisers who need hosts, presenters or VIP-event staff.
  • Companies that want on-camera talent in sponsored content.

What you get

  • Talent types: elite ambassadors, commercial and fashion models, actors and on-camera talent, hosts and presenters, hostesses and VIP staff.
  • A shortlist with portfolios for your date, city and headcount.
  • Briefing on product, messaging and event flow.
  • Day-of management through one point of contact.
  • Clear terms on usage rights and professional conduct.
  • Talent names and photos shown only with each person’s consent.

How it works

  1. Request

    Share the date, city, talent type, headcount and how the footage will be used.

  2. Shortlist

    We send portfolios that fit the brief for your approval.

  3. Brief

    We brief the talent and confirm schedule, wardrobe and usage rights.

  4. Event day

    A coordinator manages the talent from arrival to wrap.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between elite talent and standard ambassadors?

Elite talent covers premium, on-camera and VIP work such as models, actors and hosts. Standard ambassadors cover promotions, sampling and booth work.

Can we see portfolios?

Yes, we share portfolios with consent for your specific brief. We do not publish talent names or photos publicly.

What about usage rights?

Usage rights are agreed in writing before the booking and affect the price.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is on request. Draft pricing starts from $90 per person per day and varies with tier, usage rights and duration.

Can talent work outside Karachi?

Yes, in Lahore and Islamabad, subject to availability.
