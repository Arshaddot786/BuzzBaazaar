Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who we are

BuzzBaazaar is a marketing company based in Karachi, Pakistan. Contact us at hello@buzzbaazaar.com.

What we collect

When you send a brief, we collect the details you enter: name, work email, company, website, role, what you want to promote, goal, target market, budget range, timeline and notes. If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, we receive the details you share. We use analytics to understand how visitors use this website.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, prepare proposals, deliver services and manage our relationship with you. We keep information only as long as needed for these purposes.

Campaign participants

When we run public campaigns, we collect participant details such as name, phone and city only with the participant’s consent and with clear disclosure of how the information will be used and shared with the sponsoring brand.

Sharing

We share information with service providers who help us run the website, send email and deliver campaigns, and with sponsoring brands where participants have consented. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing hello@buzzbaazaar.com.

This notice is a starting version and is subject to legal review.
