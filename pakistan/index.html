Frequently asked questions

Do you work with Pakistani brands?

Yes. This section is for Pakistani businesses. We run activations, content, talent, sampling and research in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad.

How is pricing handled?

Local campaigns are quoted in PKR on request, based on your brief. Share your goal, city and dates and we will send a quotation.

Is the rooftop fleet available outside Karachi?

The fleet currently runs in Karachi only.

How do we get started?

Book a call or send a brief. We recommend a campaign and send a quote, and an advance confirms the booking.

What payment terms apply?

An advance of 50% or more, depending on the programme, confirms your booking and is required before production begins. It is non-refundable once production has begun. The balance is due before activation.
