Frequently asked questions

Are these prices final?

No. They are draft indicative “from” prices. Final scope and price are confirmed on your strategy call.

Can we combine packages?

Yes. Most campaigns combine several, for example a Market Test with a Viral Content Engine plan.

How do we pay?

We invoice in USD. You can pay by Wise, international bank transfer or another international payment provider. Details are on your invoice.

What is the advance?

An advance of 50% or more, depending on the programme, confirms your booking and is required before production begins. The advance is non-refundable once production has begun.

Is the vehicle wrap included?

No. Wrap setup is a separate one-time fee per branded vehicle, quoted separately.
