Campaign photos from the field

Real photos from recent community and app-awareness activations in Karachi, taken on the day. We show real work like this in place of invented case studies.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Real campaign photos from recent activations. Faces of some attendees are softened to respect privacy. Venue and client details are not part of these captions. Some photos are small, because they come from the team’s phones on the day.

What these photos show

A community session, one-to-one conversations, on-site app demonstrations and the team on the day. They show how our field teams work with the public: briefing a group, helping people try something on their own phones and keeping the setting relaxed.

What they do not show

They are not campaign results. Reach, sign-ups and conversions are reported separately for every campaign, with the method stated. We will add case studies only when a client has agreed and we can prove the numbers.
