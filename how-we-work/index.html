Clear from day one.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Six steps

  1. Free strategy call

    About 15 minutes on call or WhatsApp. We cover your goals, audience and locations, then recommend a campaign type, duration and package. No sales pitch.

  2. Brief approved

    You confirm the campaign type, duration and locations and send your logo, brand colours and product guidelines.

  3. Production

    Print files for cups, standees, canopy panels and caps go to production partners. Wrap designs are finalised, ambassadors are briefed, quiz questions are loaded and the QR lead form is built.

  4. Booking confirmed and ready to launch

    The advance confirms your booking. Vehicles are wrapped, permissions cleared and the crew and videographer scheduled.

  5. Activation live

    The activation runs at the chosen locations with live updates. Your actual start date depends on approvals, permits and production.

  6. Report within 48 hours

    Within 48 hours of the final activation day you receive your report, and we hold a short wrap-up call.

What you provide

  • Brand goals, target audience and locations
  • Confirmation of campaign type, duration and locations
  • Logo, brand colours and product guidelines
  • Your advance to confirm the booking

What we provide

  • Campaign type and duration recommendation
  • Print production, wrap design and fitting, ambassador briefing, quiz setup and the QR lead form
  • Location permissions, a videographer and crew
  • Live WhatsApp updates during activation
  • 5 to 8 short clips per activation day for Reels, TikTok and Facebook, with your logo and music, within 48 hours, where included in scope
  • A QR lead list in Excel (name, phone, city) where consent and disclosure allow
  • A participation report with total participants, peak hours, location photos and crowd estimates with method
  • CSR documentation for heat-relief and community campaigns
  • A short wrap-up call

Payment terms

  • An advance of 50% or more, depending on the programme, confirms your booking and is required before production begins.
  • The advance covers wrap production, printing and crew scheduling, and is non-refundable once production has begun.
  • The balance is due before activation begins, on the first activation day.
  • Wrap setup is a separate one-time fee per branded vehicle.
  • We invoice in USD. Pay by Wise, international bank transfer or another international payment provider.

Communication

We work by WhatsApp and email, with live updates during activations and a weekly call for monthly retainers. Our time zone is Pakistan Standard Time (UTC+5), and we arrange calls to suit your hours.

Data handling: lead lists are shared securely and only where participants gave consent. See our privacy notice.
