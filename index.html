How it works

From a free call to a finished report. Most campaigns start 3 to 7 days after the brief is approved, depending on availability, permits and production.

  1. Strategy call

    Tell us your goal, audience and locations. We recommend a campaign, a scope and a starting price.

  2. Brief and booking

    You approve the brief and send your brand assets. An advance confirms the booking.

  3. Production and launch

    We produce, brief the team, clear permissions and run the campaign with live updates.

  4. Report

    Edited clips, photos, participation figures and leads where collected, within 48 hours of the final activation day.

Frequently asked questions

What does BuzzBaazaar do?

BuzzBaazaar is a Karachi-based below-the-line and viral marketing partner. We produce short-form and street content, run on-ground activations, supply ambassadors and talent, and run field research for global brands.

How can an international brand run a campaign in Pakistan?

Book a free strategy call. We recommend a campaign, confirm scope and price, handle permissions, staff, production and filming, and report results. You can start with a low-cost Pakistan Market Test.

What can be delivered remotely?

Short-form video, street content, sponsored social experiments, survey reports and creator campaigns are produced in Pakistan and delivered to you digitally, with no travel needed.

Can BuzzBaazaar manage on-ground activations?

Yes. We run activations in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, including sampling, quizzes, ambassadors and a rooftop-convertible fleet in Karachi.

How much does it cost?

Packages start from $450 for research, $600 a month for content and $750 for a market test. These are draft indicative prices, confirmed on your strategy call.

How quickly can we start?

Typically 3 to 7 days after the brief is approved, depending on availability, permits and production.
